One workflow, from file to finding

Move through each stage at your own pace. Keep the data, analysis, and explanation connected.

Prepare your data

Check field names and data types, then look for missing values, duplicate rows, and inconsistent units before beginning analysis.

Explore the question

Compare groups and time periods, check unusual values, and consider the dataset’s source and collection context when interpreting patterns.

Share what you found

Use a clear chart title, units, and covered period, then explain the takeaway so others can understand the result.
